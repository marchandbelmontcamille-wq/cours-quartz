Mr. Musk,

In interviews, you say that the fastest way to make Mars inhabitable for humans is to drop thermonuclear bombs over the poles.

According to you, the bombs would change and warm the Martian climate with greenhouse gas liberation. Your final but is the terraformation of Mars for a backup if the Earth is no inhabitable because global warming. It's a good project.

But the problem is that the reactions of the detonations on the Martian atmosphere are not yet understood. We don't know if the entire planet will be made radioactive indefinitely, if the atmosphere will become dangerously hot, or if hypothetical living organisms would be killed by the thermonuclear bombs.

The terraformation of Mars may one day be one of the greatest human's achievements but only with the caution and scientific rigor required.
