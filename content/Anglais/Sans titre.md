## Video 2
The video is a press conference given by Elon Musk about his project to send people to Mars and settle there. He answers two journalists' questions about the journey and subsequent life on the red planet.
He depicts the hardships people will have to overcome : they may die during the journey and will be exposed to radiations
To protect them, he suggests to orient the spacecraft away from the sun. Although according to him they might still get cancer. 
He seems optimistic about his project. Even though many people would die, he believes that the risks are worth it.
However, his hesitation and responses show that he probably didn't think this through.
